# Legacy v1.9.4 firmware downloads

This page provides **v1.9.4** only, for maintaining v1 devices. For new devices, prefer [v2.6.0](./v2.6.0.md) or [online building and flashing](../online-builder.md).

<a name="scope"></a>

## Scope {#scope}

Identify why v1 must be retained, and confirm board, chip, and partition layout. See the [official v1.9.4 release](https://github.com/78/xiaozhi-esp32/releases/tag/v1.9.4) for notes and source. Even identically named boards may use different v1/v2 partitions; changing a download filename does not make direct OTA suitable.

<a name="download"></a>

## Find firmware by board {#download}

These **88 packages** are grouped by product family. Check product, chip, and variant before clicking the complete filename, or search by model with Ctrl+F / ⌘F. After extracting, follow [Image types and write addresses](../flashing.md#image-types). Source archives are not device firmware.
